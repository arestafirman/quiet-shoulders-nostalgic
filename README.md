One sentence — a childhood memory that makes the AI judge feel nostalgic — for the
[Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory](https://make.fun/c/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363)
bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes the sentence deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every August evening my father carried me home asleep on his shoulder, my cheek against his warm shirt, past the warung with the blue door, and he walked the last two hundred metres slower so the crickets could finish their song before he laid me down on the mat.

No network, no randomness, no dependencies — the program writes one fixed
sentence and exits.

## Why this sentence

A held scene, no twist: every August evening the father carries the child
home asleep on his shoulder. The body sensation is the cheek against his
warm shirt; the second, literal beat is the last two hundred metres walked
slower so the crickets can finish their song — a named distance, a named
place (the warung with the blue door), a sound. It closes on the mat, on
the act of being laid down, not on a summary. 263 characters, one sentence.
Verified locally: two runs produce byte-identical output, 263 characters /
263 bytes, exactly one full stop, no trailing newline.
